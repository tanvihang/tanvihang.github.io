/ About Days Left

Privacy Policy Terms of Service Support

Days Left is a minimal countdown app that remembers how you feel, not just how many days remain.

Create an event, choose a hand-drawn mood or draw your own, and optionally add a few words. Check in again whenever your feelings change.

Look back through each event’s timeline to rediscover the anticipation, doubts and little moments that made it yours.

/ The Problem

A countdown tells us when something will happen. It rarely remembers how we felt along the way.

The days before an event are part of the memory, too.
