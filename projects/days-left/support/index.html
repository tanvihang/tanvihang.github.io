Getting started

  1. Create an event and choose the date or date range you are counting down to.
  2. Record how you feel by choosing a hand-drawn mood. Add a note if you want to keep a few words about the moment.
  3. Return to the event to record another feeling whenever you want.
  4. Look back through the event’s timeline to see how your feelings changed.

You can also draw your own moods and event illustrations to make the experience more personal.

Common questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No Days Left account is needed. Your countdowns and mood entries work locally on your device. Opening these support pages or sending an email needs an internet connection.

Where are my events, moods and drawings stored?

They are stored locally on your device. Days Left does not provide a cloud synchronization or backup service, and the developer cannot see your private entries. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can you recover missing entries or move them to another device?

We do not hold a server copy of your entries and cannot restore them remotely. Whether they can be restored from an iOS or computer backup depends on your backup settings. Please contact us before deleting or reinstalling the app while troubleshooting.

How do I remove my data?

Deleting Days Left from your device removes its local app data. Offloading the app may retain that data. Any copies in iCloud or computer backups must be managed separately through your device or backup settings. There is no Days Left cloud account to delete.

Do I have to draw every mood myself?

No. Choose one of the ready-made hand-drawn moods for a quick check-in, or draw your own when you want a more personal expression. Written notes are optional too.

Is Days Left free?

The initial App Store release is planned as a free download. Additional optional tools may be offered as in-app purchases in a future version. Any paid option will show its price before you purchase it.

Reporting a problem

To help investigate, include:

  • Your device model, iOS version and Days Left app version, if available.
  • The steps that led to the problem.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if useful, with any private information hidden.

You do not need to share your personal journal entries. Support is handled by the independent developer, without a guaranteed response time.

Feedback and privacy requests

Send feature suggestions, accessibility feedback or privacy questions to byatpical@gmail.com. For a privacy request, please use the subject Days Left Privacy.
