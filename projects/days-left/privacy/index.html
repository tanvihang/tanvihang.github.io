Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Your countdowns and feelings stay on your device. Days Left stores your events, mood entries, notes and drawings locally. We do not operate a cloud account, backup or synchronization service for this content.

1. Who we are

Days Left is developed and operated by Vi Hang Tan (“we”, “us” or “our”). This Privacy Policy covers the Days Left mobile app and these Privacy Policy, Terms of Service and Support pages. For privacy questions, contact byatpical@gmail.com.

2. Information stored in the app

The information you create in Days Left stays in the app’s local storage on your device. This includes:

  • Event names, dates, countdowns and appearance settings.
  • Mood selections, the dates of your entries and optional written notes.
  • Drawings you create for events or moods.

The app uses this information on your device to display your countdowns, save your entries and show each event’s timeline. You do not need a Days Left account. We do not receive your journal content, and the app does not upload it to servers operated by us.

3. Data collection and sharing

Days Left does not send us personal information, app-usage analytics or crash-report data through a cloud service. We do not sell or rent your personal information, use your entries for advertising, or track you across other apps and websites.

If you choose to send information outside the app, such as attaching a screenshot to a support email, the information you send is handled by the service you choose and as described below.

4. Storage, backups and deletion

Your local content remains on your device until it is removed. Deleting Days Left from your device removes its local app data; merely offloading the app may keep that data. We cannot access or restore a copy of your entries on our servers because we do not hold one.

Depending on your device and backup settings, iOS may include app data in an iCloud or computer backup. These are system-level backups managed by you and Apple, rather than a Days Left cloud service. Copies in those backups must be managed separately through your device or backup settings.

Your device’s security settings help protect local content. Please consider the effect on your entries before deleting the app or changing devices.

5. When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to reply, investigate the issue and maintain the necessary support correspondence. You do not need to send your private mood notes or drawings to receive help.

Support emails are handled through Gmail. Google processes email according to its Privacy Policy. Support correspondence may be processed outside your country and is kept only as long as reasonably needed to handle your request or meet legal obligations.

6. These web pages and external services

These three information pages do not embed advertising, analytics or tracking scripts, and do not read your Days Left app data. They are hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as your IP address, to deliver and secure the pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Apple operates the App Store and device backup services under its own terms and Privacy Policy. Following a link to another website, including the separate portfolio pages, is subject to that site’s data practices.

7. Your choices and rights

You control the content you enter and your device settings. Because your app content is local, requests to us cannot retrieve or erase that content remotely.

For personal information you have sent in a support email, you may contact us to request access, correction or deletion, or to exercise other rights available under applicable law. We may retain information where required by law. Where applicable, you may also raise a concern with your local data-protection authority.

We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the app. If a child has sent personal information to our support address, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its removal.

8. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its date if our privacy practices change. Any future feature that changes how personal information is handled will be reflected in this policy and any notices or consent required by applicable law.

Privacy contact: Vi Hang Tan · byatpical@gmail.com.
