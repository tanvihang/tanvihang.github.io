/ About OM Film Lab

Visit OM Film Lab ↗

OM Film Lab brings camera recipes, visual discovery and photo memories into one dedicated app.

Built for photographers who love shooting straight out of camera (SOOC), it helps users find a look they love without a separate editing workflow.

Discover recipes, keep track of camera settings, and give the memories made with your camera a home of their own.
